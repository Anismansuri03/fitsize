# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Fitsize's community to be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of
experience level, background, identity, or anything else.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what's best for the project and its users

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour can be reported by opening a GitHub issue marked private-sensitive
where possible, or by contacting the maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. All reports
will be reviewed and result in a response appropriate to the circumstances, up to and including a
temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces.
